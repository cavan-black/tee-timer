Tee Timer — Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 July 2026

Tee Timer shows you the tee times and green fee prices that golf clubs between Sotogrande and Fuengirola are publishing on their own booking systems right now. It is a paid app with no advertising. This policy explains, plainly, what it does and does not do with information.

The short version

Tee Timer has no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising. It does not ask for your name, email address or location, and it does not build a profile of you. It cannot take a payment or make a booking, so it never sees payment details.

What leaves your phone

When you tap Find tee times, the app sends your search — the date, the time of day, the number of players, 18 or 9 holes, and any areas you picked — to our server, which reads the clubs' booking systems and sends back the results. That request carries no name, no account and no device identifier. Two searches from the same phone are not linked to each other.

Server logs

Our API runs on Vercel. Like any web server it records ordinary request logs, which include the IP address the request came from, briefly and automatically. We use these only to keep the service running and to spot abuse. We do not analyse them to identify anyone, and we do not combine them with anything else. Vercel is our hosting processor; see Vercel's privacy policy.

What stays on your phone

Your language, your light or dark preference, whether you have seen the introduction, and a short-lived copy of your most recent search results are stored on the device itself so the app works offline and opens the way you left it. None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, ad networks or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location access. Areas are chosen by tapping, not sensed.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is nothing to sell.

When you leave the app

Tapping a tee time opens that golf club's own booking page, and tapping Call dials the clubhouse using your phone's dialler. From that point you are dealing with the club, under the club's own privacy policy and terms. Tee Timer takes no payment, holds no reservation and is not a party to your booking.

Children

Tee Timer is a golf booking tool for general audiences. It is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR gives you rights of access, correction, erasure and objection over personal data held about you. In practice we hold no information that identifies you, so in most cases there is nothing to retrieve or erase. If you believe otherwise, write to us and we will look into it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
